PROBLEM 1 LEATHER TURNING DRUM

Leather Turning Drum Design

Design a working leather turning drum for tannery operations. The drum must address four critical failure modes:

  • Leakage - Fluid loss during rotation cycles
  • Corrosion - Chemical degradation from tanning solutions
  • Loading/Off-loading difficulty - Manual handling inefficiency
  • Low motor torque - Insufficient rotational force for loaded drum
